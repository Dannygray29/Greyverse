# GreyVerse — Buyer Outreach Pack

## Short pitch

GreyVerse is an early-stage football gaming competition platform built around DLS and eFootball. It already has a Next.js/React/TypeScript web application, Supabase/PostgreSQL competition architecture, player profiles, game-specific contexts, leagues, fixtures, rankings, tournaments, match verification structures, rewards and operational tooling.

The asset is available for acquisition by a founder, developer, gaming community or esports operator who wants an existing technical foundation instead of starting from zero.

**Asking:** $4,900 USD OBO

## Buyer qualification questions

Before discussing transfer, ask:

1. What do you intend to build with GreyVerse?
2. Are you interested in source code only or the broader GreyVerse product/IP asset?
3. Do you require a technical handover?
4. Do you require any post-sale development?
5. What transaction method do you propose?
6. Are you prepared to perform technical due diligence before closing?

## Negotiation position

- Public asking price: **$4,900 OBO**
- Preferred negotiated zone: **$2,000–$3,500**
- Do not voluntarily disclose the $1,000 floor.
- Any offer below the target zone should be evaluated against the requested scope and handover obligations.
- Do not transfer repository ownership, credentials, secrets or production assets before cleared payment and written transaction terms.

## What not to claim

Do not describe GreyVerse as:

- revenue-generating
- having paying customers
- having a large active user base
- fully production-ready
- fully tested end-to-end
- guaranteed to generate revenue

Those claims are not established by the current repository.
